Privacy Policy

VB Comment DM · Last updated: October 23, 2026

VB Comment DM ("the App") is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the Instagram account @vanbx. It sends a direct message to people who comment a specific keyword on that account's posts, so they receive the link or resource they asked for.

Information the App processes

  • Comments posted on the account's own posts: comment text, comment ID, and the commenter's Instagram username and ID.
  • The ID of the post the comment was left on.

The App does not collect passwords, payment information, or any data from other parts of a user's Instagram profile.

How the information is used

Only to (1) detect whether a comment contains a configured keyword, (2) send one automated reply message to that commenter, and (3) optionally reply publicly to the comment. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or sold or shared with third parties.

Storage and retention

The App stores only comment IDs (to avoid sending duplicate messages) and a short activity log on the operator's own server. Message content is not stored beyond that log. Logs are deleted on request.

Third parties

The App communicates only with Meta's Instagram API. Data is subject to Meta's own policies. No other third party receives your data.

Your choices and data deletion

You can stop receiving messages by not commenting keywords, or by blocking the account. To request deletion of any stored data related to you, email the address below and we will delete it within 30 days. You can also remove the App's access in Instagram under Settings → Website permissions.
